RobloxGuard Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 13, 2026

Who this is for

RobloxGuard is used by parents and legal guardians, not children. The child never installs or logs into the app.

We do not collect: Roblox password, private chat content, location, advertising identifier, health/financial/biometric data.

What we don't do

  • No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • No sale of data.
  • No reading of private Roblox chats — detection uses only public, unauthenticated Roblox web APIs.

How long we keep it

Data for a linked child is retained while the account stays linked. Unlinking a child in Settings permanently and immediately deletes all associated data.

Who can see it

Only you. Backend access is restricted to the operator via a bearer-token API key. Bug reports (and any contact email) are visible to the developer only for fixing the issue.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

RobloxGuard is not directed at children and is not in Apple's Kids Category. It is installed and operated exclusively by the parent/guardian.

Changes

We will update the effective date above for material changes.

Contact: Settings → Support in the app.
