ChadDrop Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 4, 2026

Default Offline App

ChadDrop does not require an account. In its default offline configuration, pasted text is processed on the device by the built-in rule engine.

If AI Is Enabled

If the app is configured to use an AI proxy, pasted text may be sent to that proxy only to generate the requested decode result. The proxy should not log raw pasted messages.

Tracking

ChadDrop does not use third-party advertising or tracking.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support contact listed on the Support page.
