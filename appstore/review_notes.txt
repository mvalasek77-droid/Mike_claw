2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
BoxCall is a free-to-play prediction game for movie fans aged 13+. Players predict how much upcoming films will earn on their opening weekend by buying virtual "Call" (bigger) or "Put" (smaller) contracts with Reel Coins, a play currency. It turns box-office tracking news into a game and teaches basic options concepts with no financial risk.
Reel Coins cannot be bought as a balance, cashed out, transferred between players, or exchanged for anything of value. Weekly cycle: trading on a movie locks when it opens Friday; on Sunday each account's weekly stake is taken back and profit is kept, and that afternoon results settle; on Monday every account gets a fresh stake (1,000 RC free). Subscriptions (Backstage, Producer's Pass, Mogul) add a name badge, early market access, limit orders, stats and custom markets; they never add Reel Coins, cash or prizes.

3. HOW TO USE THE APP
No login or demo account is needed. The whole app works as a guest.
- Launch, enter any birth year 13+ years ago, swipe through the 8-page tour (or Skip).
- Now Showing tab: tap any movie, choose BIGGER (Call) or SMALLER (Put), tap a strike row, set quantity, tap Buy.
- Positions tab: open trades with live P&L; Close sells a position early.
- Positions settle automatically on the Sunday of each opening weekend, using the studios' reported domestic opening estimate.
- Box Office tab: leaderboard ranked by total trading profit. Marquee tab: the top 5's movie reviews, then the feed.
- Profile: membership (Upgrade opens the paywall), Sign in with Apple (optional), Replay the app tour, Terms, Privacy, and Delete account and data.
Account deletion: Profile > Delete account and data > Delete. This erases all trades, coins, rank, posts, reviews and sign-in details and returns the app to its first-launch state. All account data is stored only on the device; there is no server-side account.
User-generated content: posts, comments and reviews are stored on the device only. Every item offers Report, Block user and Not interested (hide). Other traders shown in the feed and leaderboard are simulated rivals generated by the app.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Apple: StoreKit 2 (subscriptions), Sign in with Apple (optional), local notifications, WidgetKit, Live Activities, Apple Watch.
- GitHub Pages: hosts public JSON files with the movie slate and reported opening-weekend grosses, compiled from public box-office reports (Box Office Mojo, The Numbers).
- TMDB API (optional): movie posters and metadata when enabled.
- YouTube: trailer links.
No analytics SDKs, ad networks, AI services or third-party payment processors are used, and no personal data leaves the device.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app works the same in every region where it is available. Content is in English and uses U.S. domestic box-office figures everywhere.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
BoxCall is not in a regulated industry. No real money is wagered or won, nothing of value can be won, and it is not a securities or event-contract product; "Call", "Put" and "strike" are game terms. Movie titles, release dates and cast names are used as factual references. Posters, when shown, come from TMDB under its API terms with the required attribution (Profile > Data sources). BoxCall is not affiliated with any studio.
