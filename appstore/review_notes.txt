NOTES TO APP REVIEW

This app is a PLAY-MONEY social prediction game — the same category as ESPN Fantasy Football's pick'em, Manifold Markets, and Metaculus. It is not gambling, not a securities offering, and not an event-contract exchange.

Play-money mechanics:
  - Users buy virtual "Call" and "Put" contracts on the opening-weekend
    domestic box-office grosses of upcoming movies, using an in-app
    virtual currency ("Reel Coins").
  - Reel Coins CANNOT be purchased as a balance. Subscriptions grant a
    bonus amount and a larger weekly refill, but the bonus is denominated
    in the play currency and cannot be redeemed for cash, prizes, real
    goods, or any consideration of value.
  - Reel Coins CANNOT be transferred between users. No trading, no gifting.
  - Winning contracts pay play currency at Monday settlement based on the
    reported opening-weekend gross from Box Office Mojo. No real prize.

App Store Guideline 5.3 (Gaming, Gambling & Lotteries) — we believe the
app is compliant because none of the criteria for gambling apply: no
real-money wagering, no cash prizes, no real property or thing of value.
See ESPN Fantasy Football / DraftKings' free-to-play tier / Manifold
Markets as precedent for the same design.

Age rating: 12+ because the app includes user-generated content (feed
posts, reviews, comments). Full moderation UI is mandatory-visible on
every user-generated item: Report (with reason picker and optional block),
Block user, Hide item. Blocked handles never appear in feed, reviews, or
comments. Report queue is server-side.

Test account: Not required — the entire app is usable in guest mode
without sign-in. Sign in with Apple is optional and only used for
cross-device sync.

Demo mode notes:
  - The paywall includes a demo fallback that activates a subscription
    even when StoreKit hasn't loaded products, so App Review can
    exercise every tier without configuring StoreKit Configuration.
  - "Simulate opening weekends" button in the Portfolio tab triggers
    settlement on a random opening within ±IV of the tracking estimate.

Data sources: TMDB (client-side), plus server-side aggregation of IMDb
Coming Soon, The Numbers, Deadline, and Box Office Mojo. All references
to third-party trademarks are informational.

Privacy: Comprehensive Privacy Policy shipped in-app under Profile → Privacy.
No third-party ad networks. No cross-app tracking. Anonymous analytics only.
