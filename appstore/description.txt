Trade the opening weekend.

BoxCall is an options chain for movies about to open. Buy CALLs when you think a movie will beat tracking. Buy PUTs when you smell a bomb. Every account starts with 1,000 Reel Coins. Winning gets you status, not cash.

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT
Not a poll. Not a prediction market. A full continuous-payoff options chain with five strikes above and below the current implied consensus, live 24/7. Market makers work rolling support and resistance so the tape looks like a real chart, not a random walk.

THE LIVE MARKET
Every contract has a mark that moves in real time. Your buys and sells shift the price. News events (tracking updates, reviews, trailer spikes) shock the whole chain. The implied opening estimate — the "consensus" — is a live crowd forecast derived from base tracking, YouTube trailer engagement, X mention velocity, and actual trade flow.

WIN STATUS, NOT CASH
Six reputation tiers from Rookie to Oracle. Badges for feats: Bomb Caller, Rocket, Contrarian, Sniper. Weekly streaks. Season titles you keep forever. The top-5 traders each week get their movie reviews spotlighted at the top of the Feed — winning literally becomes editorial voice.

MONDAY RESET
Every account gets a fresh weekly allowance every Monday at midnight local time. Free tier is 500 Reel Coins per week, forever. Lose your last coin? Trading pauses. Come back Monday.

SUBSCRIPTIONS UNLOCK MORE COINS, NOT MORE STATUS
Three optional tiers add more Reel Coins and quality-of-life perks. Leaderboards, badges, and Featured Critics slots stay earned.

WHAT'S IN THE APP
• Slate — every upcoming release with its live implied opening and consensus sparkline
• Chain — five CALL and five PUT strikes per movie with live marks and S/R lines
• Feed — shared calls from the community, with likes, comments, follow, and copy-trade
• Featured Critics — top-5 traders each week get their movie reviews at the top of the Feed
• Portfolio — open positions with live P&L, working limit orders, and one-tap close
• Leaderboard — season-long ranking with color-coded tiers
• Home-screen widget — top position and next opening on your lock screen

NOT REAL MONEY
Reel Coins are play-money. They cannot be purchased as a balance, cannot be redeemed for cash, cannot be transferred. Nothing on BoxCall is a securities offering, event contract, or wager. BoxCall is entertainment.

DATA SOURCES
Upcoming releases from TMDB (in-app) plus IMDb Coming Soon and The Numbers (server-side). Settlement grosses from Box Office Mojo Monday mornings. Tracking numbers from Deadline / NRG. Full attribution in the Data Sources screen.

PRIVACY
No data selling. No ad networks. No cross-app tracking. Optional Sign in with Apple for cross-device sync. Anonymous product analytics only.
