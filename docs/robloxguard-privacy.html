RobloxGuard Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 15, 2026

Who this is for

RobloxGuard is used by parents and legal guardians, not children. The child never installs or logs into the app. This policy describes what we collect from the parent using the app and, necessarily, a limited amount of information about the linked child's public Roblox account.

What we collect

DataWhyLinked to you
Your nameRecorded as the parental consent attestationYes
Your email addressOptional, for bug-report replies onlyYes, if provided
Your child's public Roblox username, display name, and account IDTo monitor the account's public footprintYes
Public friend-list changes, account-age signals, and presence timestampsCore detection functionalityYes

Evidence you upload (screenshots) and captured public profile screenshotsTo preserve observations for reportingYes
Subscription statusApple's StoreKit tells us which plan is active; we never see your card numberYes

We do not collect: your child's Roblox password, private chat content, your location, your device's advertising identifier, or health, financial, or biometric data.

What we don't do

  • No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • No selling of data, ever.
  • No reading of private Roblox chats — detection runs on public, unauthenticated web APIs only.

How long we keep it

Data for a linked child is retained while the account stays linked. Unlinking a child in Settings permanently and immediately deletes everything associated with that child.

Who can see it

Only you. Backend access is restricted to the operator via a bearer-token API key. Bug reports (and any contact email you provide) are visible to the developer only for fixing the issue.

Your rights

You can request deletion at any time by unlinking the child's account in the app. For questions, contact mvalasek77@gmail.com.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

RobloxGuard is not directed at children and is not available in Apple's Kids Category. It is installed and operated exclusively by the parent/guardian. Information about a linked child is limited to what's necessary for the service and is provided under the parent's consent.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the effective date above.
