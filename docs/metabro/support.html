Frequently Asked Questions

What is MetaBro?
MetaBro is a social networking app that combines the best of social media and community forums — designed for men who want authentic connections, meaningful conversations, and real communities (Bro-hoods).
How do I create an account?
Open MetaBro and follow the onboarding flow. Choose a handle (3–20 characters, lowercase letters/digits/underscores) and a display name. That's it — no email or phone verification required.
What are Bro-hoods?
Bro-hoods are communities organized around shared interests — fitness, grilling, cars, fatherhood, philosophy, and more. Join as many as you want. Each has its own feed, moderators, and events.
What is Bro Cred?
Bro Cred is your combined reputation score from post and comment karma. You earn it when other users upvote your content or give you awards.
What is the Bro-ket?
The Bro-ket is MetaBro's built-in marketplace where you can buy, sell, or trade gear with other bros. Contact between buyers and sellers happens through in-app messaging — your personal info is never exposed.
How do I report or block someone?
Tap the ··· menu on any post, comment, profile, or message. You can report content for review, mute a user (hide their content from your feed), or block them (prevent all interaction).
How do I export an event to my calendar?
Open any event in the Events section and tap the calendar export button. MetaBro will ask for calendar permission the first time. We only write events — we never read your calendar.
How do I delete my account?
Go to your Profile tab and tap "Sign out." To permanently delete your account and all associated data, email us at mvalasek77@gmail.com. We'll process the deletion within 30 days.
Does MetaBro work on iPad?
Yes. MetaBro features an adaptive layout that uses a sidebar navigation on iPad and standard tab navigation on iPhone.
Is MetaBro free?
Yes, MetaBro is free to use. There are no ads, subscriptions, or in-app purchases at this time.
I found a bug. How do I report it?
Go to your Profile tab and tap "Report a problem." You can describe the issue, set a severity level, and optionally include device diagnostics (model, OS version, app version). Diagnostics never include your messages or post content.
