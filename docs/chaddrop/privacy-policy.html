PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 9, 2026

Alpha Elite Holdings Inc. ("we," "us," or "our") built the ChadDrop application (the "App") as a privacy-first product. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle — or, more accurately, don't handle — your data when you use ChadDrop.

1. Our Simple Promise

ChadDrop is designed with a core principle: your text stays yours. The App analyzes messages for sarcasm, deception, hidden meanings, and emotional subtext entirely on-device or through ephemeral AI processing. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal information.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal data. Specifically:

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any contact information.
  • We do not collect, store, or log the text messages you analyze.
  • We do not collect device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or analytics tokens.
  • We do not collect location data.
  • We do not collect usage statistics, crash logs, or behavioral data.

3. Third-Party Services

ChadDrop does not integrate with any third-party services that could compromise your privacy:

  • No third-party analytics — We use no analytics SDKs (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel).
  • No advertising networks — The App contains no ads and uses no ad SDKs.
  • No tracking frameworks — We employ no tracking pixels, device fingerprinting, or attribution tools.
  • No social sharing — The App has no social login or sharing integrations that transmit data externally.

4. AI Processing

ChadDrop uses AI capabilities to analyze text. When text is processed:

  • Processing may occur on-device or through a secure, ephemeral API call.
  • Any text sent for analysis is not stored by our servers or our AI providers beyond the duration needed to return a result.
  • No analyzed text is used to train, improve, or enrich any machine learning model.
  • Results of analysis are returned to you and not persisted anywhere outside your device.

5. Data Retention

Because we do not collect personal data, there is nothing to retain. Any text you input into ChadDrop remains on your device and is under your full control. You may clear it at any time by deleting the App or its local data.

6. Children's Privacy

ChadDrop does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Since we collect no data at all, our practices are inherently compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations.

7. International Users

Because ChadDrop does not collect, process, or transmit personal data, it is consistent with the principles of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and other international privacy frameworks. There is no personal data to access, correct, delete, export, or port.

8. Security

While no system can guarantee absolute security, the most effective safeguard is not collecting data in the first place. ChadDrop's architecture minimizes risk by ensuring that personal data never reaches our systems. Any on-device data is protected by the security features of your device's operating system.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change how ChadDrop handles data, we will update this Privacy Policy and revise the "Effective Date" above. Any material changes will be communicated through the App or other appropriate channels. We will never silently begin collecting data.
