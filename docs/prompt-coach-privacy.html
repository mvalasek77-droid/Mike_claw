Claude Prompt Coach — Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 20, 2026

The short version

Prompt Coach is a paid, one-time-purchase app with no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Everything you type stays on your iPhone. The app makes no network requests at all — coaching runs entirely on device.

What we collect

We collect nothing on our own servers — we don't operate any. Everything below lives only on your device:

DataWhere it livesSent to us
The prompts you write and their rewritten versionsOn device (History tab)No
Your chosen target model and coaching historyOn deviceNo

What we don't do

  • No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of data, ever.
  • No user accounts, sign-ins, or profiles.
  • No collection of location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or device fingerprints.

Payments

Claude Prompt Coach is a one-time purchase on the App Store with no in-app purchases and no subscriptions. Apple processes your payment; we never receive your name, email, or payment details.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Your control

Because all data is on your device, you control it completely: clear individual history entries or all of them from within the app, or delete the app to remove everything.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here and in the app under Settings → Legal, with a new effective date.
