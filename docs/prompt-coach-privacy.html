Claude Prompt Coach — Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 20, 2026

The short version

Claude Prompt Coach is a paid, one-time-purchase app with no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Everything you type stays on your iPhone. The only time any of your text leaves the device is if you choose to use the optional Test It feature, which sends the polished prompt to Anthropic using an API key you supply.

What we collect

We collect nothing on our own servers — we don't operate any. Everything below lives only on your device:

DataWhere it livesSent to us
The prompts you write and their rewritten versionsOn device (History tab)No
Your chosen target model and coaching historyOn deviceNo
Your Anthropic API key (optional)iOS Keychain on deviceNo — used only for direct calls to Anthropic when you tap Test It

The optional Test It feature

If you paste your own Anthropic API key, the app can run a polished prompt against the Claude model you selected so you can compare quality. When you do this:

  • Your key is stored in the iOS Keychain and never leaves the device except in the request to Anthropic's API.
  • The prompt and Anthropic's reply are handled under Anthropic's privacy policy and your own Anthropic account terms.
  • You are billed by Anthropic directly for that usage — we never see your key's billing or your card.

The app is fully usable without a key. Coaching itself runs entirely on device and needs no network connection.

What we don't do

  • No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of data, ever.
  • No user accounts, sign-ins, or profiles.
  • No collection of location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or device fingerprints.

Payments

Claude Prompt Coach is a one-time purchase on the App Store with no in-app purchases and no subscriptions. Apple processes your payment; we never receive your name, email, or payment details.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Your control

Because all data is on your device, you control it completely: clear individual history entries or all of them from within the app, remove your API key at any time, or delete the app to remove everything.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here and in the app under Settings → Legal, with a new effective date.
