Delete your Auction Baby account

Auction Baby · developer: Auction Baby · mvalasek77@gmail.com

Delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Auction Baby on your phone (Android or iPhone) or on the web.
  2. Tap You in the bottom bar.
  3. Tap Delete account permanently and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Don't have the app installed?

You can delete your account from any browser — no download needed:

  1. Go to mvalasek77-droid.github.io/Mike_claw/auctionbaby/app.
  2. Sign in with the same email and password (or Sign in with Apple) you used in the app.
  3. Tap You → Delete account permanently and confirm.

Can't sign in? Email mvalasek77@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject “Delete my Auction Baby account”. We'll confirm it's you and delete the account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: name, email, password and date of birth
  • Your profile, photos, bio, prompts and interests
  • Bids you placed or received, matches, and all chat messages
  • Your blocks, reports you filed, and notification settings

What we keep, and for how long

Records of purchases (such as Gavel packs or date-reservation fees) may be kept for as long as the law requires for tax and accounting, and payment providers keep their own transaction records. We may also keep limited information needed to prevent fraud or abuse, or to comply with a legal request. Everything else is removed when you delete your account.
