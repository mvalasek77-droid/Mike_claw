Common questions

What is a “bid”?

A bid is a letter of intent — the budget you’re committing to spend on the date itself (dinner, drinks, the evening). It is never a payment to another person. Auction Baby has no way to send money between users.

How do I stay safe?

Meet in public for a first date, tell a friend where you’ll be, and never send money to anyone you meet. Use Report & Block on anyone who feels off — they’re removed from your experience and can’t contact you.

What does the blue check mean?

A verified profile matched a live selfie to its photos. It confirms a real person behind the profile; it is not a background check or a guarantee of character.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Auction Baby Pass is billed through your Apple ID. Manage or cancel any time: iPhone Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge.

What are Gavels?

Gavels are in-app currency for status features (Gilded Bids, Bid Insurance, streak freezes). They have no cash value and are never a payment to another user.

How do I delete my account or data?

In the app: Settings → Delete Account. This removes your profile and associated data. See our Privacy Policy for details and your rights.

I found a bug.

Use Settings → Report a bug in the app (it pre-fills your device info), or email [SUPPORT_EMAIL].
