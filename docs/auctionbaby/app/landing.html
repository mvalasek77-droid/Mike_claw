Why it's different

Real intent

A bid is a signal of effort, not a swipe. People who bid show up.

She's in control

She fields the bids and picks. No cold DMs, no noise.

Status, not scams

Gavels and a Pass unlock perks. Money never changes hands between people.

A bid is a promise, not a payment. It's the budget you commit to spend on the in-person date — the meal, the drinks, the night. Auction Baby never transfers money between users, by design. For adults 18+.
