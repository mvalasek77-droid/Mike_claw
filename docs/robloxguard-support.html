Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to install anything on my child's device?

No. RobloxGuard runs entirely on YOUR phone. It monitors your child's public Roblox account footprint — friend list, friends' profiles, online status — which is visible without any access to their device or password. Nothing to install, nothing for them to delete.

Can RobloxGuard read my child's chats?

No — and no honest app can. Private Roblox chats are visible only to Roblox's own moderators. That's why reporting in-platform matters: Roblox can see the messages and act on them. Any product claiming to read private chats is violating Roblox's Terms of Use.

How do I link my child's account?

Tap + on the Dashboard, enter your child's public Roblox username (never a password), and confirm you're their parent or guardian. Monitoring starts immediately: a baseline scan first, then automatic re-checks around the clock.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your iPhone, or tap "Manage subscription" in RobloxGuard's Settings. You can cancel anytime. Your coverage continues until the end of the current billing period.

How do I delete all data for a linked child?

Go to Settings → Linked accounts → Unlink → confirm. This permanently and immediately deletes all alerts, evidence, and history for that child. This cannot be undone.

What if an alert is wrong?

Alerts can be wrong in both directions — false positives and false negatives. When you tap an alert, you can mark it "Not relevant for us" (three dismissals of the same type mute it) or "This was a real problem" (switches to heightened monitoring). Your feedback tunes future alerts.

Is my data shared with third parties?

No. We don't use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and we never sell data. We store only what's necessary: your child's Roblox username, generated safety alerts, and evidence you choose to save. See our Privacy Policy.

What are the subscription prices?

Single Child: $3.99/month or $34/year. Family (up to 5 children): $8.99/month or $69/year. All subscriptions auto-renew and can be cancelled anytime.

Reporting Safety Concerns

If you believe your child is being targeted:

  • NCMEC CyberTipline — Report child exploitation to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children
  • Report to Roblox — Roblox's official reporting tool
  • Call 911 if your child is in immediate danger
