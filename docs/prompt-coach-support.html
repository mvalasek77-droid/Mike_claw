Prompt Coach — Support

Covers both Prompt Coach and Prompt Coach Lite.

Contact

Email mvalasek77@gmail.com for any question, bug report, or feedback. We aim to reply within a few business days.

Do I need an account?

No. Neither app has accounts, sign-in, or a server of any kind. Everything runs on your device.

What's the difference between Prompt Coach and Prompt Coach Lite?

They're the same core app. Prompt Coach Lite is free and coaches for two models (Claude Haiku 4.5 and Claude Sonnet 5), keeps your 3 most recent sessions, and doesn't include Sharpen, the token/cost estimate, or adaptive controls. Prompt Coach is a one-time purchase that unlocks all 5 models, unlimited history, Sharpen, cost estimates, and adaptive controls that adjust to how you work. Lite links to the Prompt Coach App Store page; nothing about that tap is sent to us.

How do I get a refund?

All payments are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com or through Settings → [your name] → Media & Purchases on your device. We can't issue refunds directly and don't have access to your payment information.

Does the app collect my data?

No. See the Prompt Coach Privacy Policy or the Prompt Coach Lite Privacy Policy.

Does this app come from Anthropic?

No. Prompt Coach and Prompt Coach Lite are independent apps that help you write prompts for Anthropic's Claude models. They are not published, endorsed, or reviewed by Anthropic.

Something's not working

Email mvalasek77@gmail.com with your device model, iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened, and we'll look into it.
