What it is

AI Marketplace is a storefront for novels, music, and film made with AI. We are not the author — independent creators are. We are the storefront and the review service. Disclosure is mandatory. Quality is enforced.

For readers, listeners, and viewers

Browse titles that have passed the AI Editor's commercial-quality bar. Every title page shows which AI systems were used. Purchases are handled by Apple in-app.

For creators

Publish novels, albums, and films you made with AI. Disclose the tools you used. Pass the 85% bar. You keep 85% of the net; AI Marketplace retains a 15% service fee. Payouts arrive in your bank by Stripe.

How payment works

Buyers pay through Apple's In-App Purchase, exactly like any other App Store app, and Apple takes its standard commission. Creators receive their 85% share of the net by direct bank deposit through Stripe Connect. We never see your card number.
