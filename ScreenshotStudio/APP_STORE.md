## Description
Screenshot Studio turns the screenshots already on your iPhone into polished,
store-ready marketing images — at exactly the pixel sizes Apple validates, so
your upload passes the first time.

• Import any screenshot — no exact sizing required.
• Frame it in a clean device bezel over a beautiful gradient or solid backdrop.
• Add a marketing headline with automatic, legible contrast.
• Export every required App Store Connect size in one tap, saved to a dedicated
  Photos album ready to upload.
• A live, pixel-accurate preview shows exactly what you'll get.

Designed with a modern Liquid Glass interface, adaptive haptics, full dark mode,
Dynamic Type, and Reduce Motion support.

Private by design: everything runs on-device. Your screenshots never leave your
iPhone. No accounts. No tracking. No analytics.

Screenshot Studio Pro is available as an auto-renewable subscription or a
one-time lifetime unlock. Monthly Pro is billed monthly. Annual Pro is billed
yearly and includes a 7-day free trial. Subscriptions renew automatically unless
cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or
cancel subscriptions in App Store account settings.
