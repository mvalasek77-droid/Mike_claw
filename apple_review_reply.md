**Guideline 5.2.5 — Legal (Intellectual Property)**
We've renamed the app from "ASC Screenshot Creator" to "Shipshot" and updated

("Store-ready screenshots, done right"). References to "App Store Connect"
that remain are strictly instructional (e.g., telling the user which Apple
service to upload their exported screenshots to) and no longer appear in the
app name, subtitle, or icon.

**Guideline 2.1(b) — Performance (App Completeness)**
We've submitted all three In-App Purchase products (monthly, annual, and
lifetime) for review along with this build, including the required App Review
screenshot for the purchase flow.

**Guideline 3.1.2(c) — Business (Payments - Subscriptions)**
We've updated the app's purchase screen to display, for each subscription
plan: its title, length, price (and per-unit price for the annual plan), and
functional links to our Terms of Use (EULA) and Privacy Policy. The same links
have been added to [App Description / EULA field] and the Privacy Policy field
in App Store Connect metadata.

**Status bar feedback**
We also addressed the reviewer's note that the synthetic status bar overlay
didn't match Apple's system status bar and didn't fully cover the screenshot's
original bar. The overlay now draws an opaque background sampled from the
screenshot's own top edge (so the original status bar is fully covered) and
automatically chooses light or dark glyphs to match, so it reads correctly
against any screenshot. We've also added a "Full-bleed screenshot" option that
exports the screenshot exactly as captured, with no device frame, background,
or overlay — a pure, unedited screenshot for users who prefer that.
