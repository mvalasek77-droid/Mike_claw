BannedTube Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

BannedTube is a local-first video platform. All your data — likes, dislikes, subscriptions, saved videos, comments, watch history, and profile — is stored exclusively on your device using iOS AsyncStorage. We do not collect, transmit, or store any data on external servers.

Data We Store

The following data is stored locally on your device:

  • Profile name — The display name you choose during onboarding
  • Watch history — Videos you've watched (max 200 entries, auto-deduplicated)
  • Liked videos — Videos you've liked
  • Disliked videos — Videos you've disliked
  • Saved videos — Videos you've bookmarked
  • Subscriptions — Channels you've subscribed to
  • Comments — Comments and replies you've posted
  • Notification preferences — Your push, dark mode, and autoplay settings
  • Recent searches — Your last 10 search queries

No Server Data Collection

BannedTube does not have a backend server. No data ever leaves your device. There are no analytics, no tracking, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party data sharing.

No Account Required

BannedTube does not require an account. Your data is tied to your device, not to an email address or identity.

Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time by:

  • Uninstalling the app — this removes all locally stored data
  • Clearing app data in iOS Settings → General → iPhone Storage → BannedTube

Children's Privacy

BannedTube is rated 17+ and is not directed at children under 17.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
