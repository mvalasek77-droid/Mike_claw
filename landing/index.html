FAQ

Is this real money?

No. Reel Coins are play-money — they can't be purchased as a balance, can't be redeemed for cash, and can't be transferred. Losing them costs nothing but pride.

Is it gambling?

No. Nothing on BoxCall is a securities offering, event contract, or wager. The play-money design mirrors ESPN Fantasy Football, not sportsbooks.

How is the opening estimate calculated?

The "implied opening" you see is a live crowd forecast — it starts from tracking numbers (Deadline / The Numbers), shifts with YouTube trailer engagement and X mention volume, and moves with actual buy / sell flow in the app.

What happens if I lose all my coins?

Trading pauses. You can still watch the market, read the feed, comment, and write reviews. On Monday your allowance lands automatically — no action needed. Subscribers get bigger bonuses.

What data powers the app?

Upcoming releases from TMDB (client-side) + IMDb Coming Soon, The Numbers, and Deadline calendars (server-side). Opening-weekend settlement from Box Office Mojo. Details on the in-app Data Sources screen.
