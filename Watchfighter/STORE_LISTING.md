## Description
THE GATE IS OPEN.

Every hundred years the tower called THE ASCENDANT wakes, and the bold climb it
floor by floor. Champions go up. None come back down. You climb anyway.

Watchfighter is a fast, original arcade fighter made for the Apple Watch — quick
sessions, big personality, real combos. Rotate the Digital Crown to move, tap to
punch and kick, and chain strikes into specials. No tiny d-pad, no fuss — just
arcade fights that fit your wrist.

• 16 original fighters, each with their own look, style, and attitude — from a
  velvet viper assassin to a war king to a near-impossible heavyweight boss.
• TOURNAMENT — a 15-floor ladder with story beats between the fights. Win to
  advance; lose and you're dragged to the Pit to fight your way back up.
• VS — single-fight challenges against the whole roster.
• LEARN — bite-size training for footwork, strikes, guard, and meter.
• Stylized hits, finishers, retro voice callouts, and an original synth soundtrack.
• The final floor: a champion who can't be beaten by damage alone. Land the
  Million Shot, or join the wall.

All characters and story are original. Built for short, addictive sessions.
