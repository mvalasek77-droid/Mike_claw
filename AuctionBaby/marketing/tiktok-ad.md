# Auction Baby — TikTok Ad

## Video Script (15-20 seconds)

### Shot 1 (0-3s) — Hook
**Visual:** Screen recording of the Auction Baby web app opening. Bold text overlay: "Apple BANNED this dating app."
**Voiceover:** "Apple banned this dating app. You're gonna wanna see why."

### Shot 2 (3-7s) — The Why
**Visual:** Quick cuts — floor screen with women's profiles, bid sheet with dollar amounts, the "Bid what a date is worth" headline.
**Text overlay:** "They said it was too disruptive."
**Voiceover:** "It's called Auction Baby. Men bid real money on dates — not on people, on the *date itself*. Dinner, drinks, the night. Highest bid wins."

### Shot 3 (7-12s) — The Value
**Visual:** Chat screen opening, match celebration, then the You page with verified blue check.
**Text overlay:** "Women field bids. They pick the winner."
**Voiceover:** "Women field the bids and accept the one they actually like. No swiping. No ghosting. Just real offers from men who put their money where their mouth is."

### Shot 4 (12-16s) — CTA
**Visual:** Screen scrolls up to show the URL bar. Big arrow points to it. Text: "Still in beta — report bugs, be first."
**Voiceover:** "Apple pulled it from the App Store. So we went web. It's still beta — there are bugs — but you can be the first to try it. Link's in the bio."

### Shot 5 (16-18s) — End card
**Visual:** Black screen, gold text: **AUCTION BABY** with the link below.
**Text:** "auctionbaby.app — Be the first to change the game forever."

## Production Notes

- **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 (vertical, 1080×1920)
- **Duration:** 18-20 seconds max (TikTok rewards short, punchy content)
- **Music:** Trending dramatic sound or the "banned/rebellious" audio trend. Search TikTok for "banned app" or "disrupt" sounds.
- **Captions:** Burn in the text overlays (don't rely on TikTok's auto-captions for the hook text)
- **Pacing:** Fast cuts every 3-4 seconds. The hook ("Apple BANNED this") must be readable in under 2 seconds.
- **Screen recording:** Record on iPhone in Safari, scrolling through the actual web app. Use iOS screen recording.
- **Text style:** Bold serif font (matches Auction Baby's brand). Gold (#D4AF37) and rose (#E0607A) accents on black background.
