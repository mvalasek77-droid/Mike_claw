## Description
```
Auction Baby is dating for people who are done with low effort.

Here, interest isn't a swipe — it's a bid. A bid is a LETTER OF INTENT: the
budget you're committing to spend on the date itself — dinner, drinks, the
whole evening. It is never a payment to another person, and Auction Baby has
no way to send money between users, by design. She reviews the intent behind
each bid — not just a photo — and accepts when it's right. Then you match, and
the conversation begins.

HOW IT WORKS
• Browse the floor — real people, real profiles.
• Place a bid: the date you're planning and what you'll put into it.
• She sees the stats and the intent, and accepts on her terms.
• Match, chat, and plan a first date worth showing up for.

BUILT FOR TRUST
• Verified profiles — a live selfie matched to real photos earns a blue check.
```
